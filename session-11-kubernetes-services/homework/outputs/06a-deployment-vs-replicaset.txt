### Scaling: I scale the Deployment, the Deployment changes the ReplicaSet's replicas
$ kubectl scale deployment web-app-clusterip --replicas=4
deployment.apps/web-app-clusterip scaled

$ kubectl rollout status deployment/web-app-clusterip --timeout=120s
Waiting for deployment "web-app-clusterip" rollout to finish: 3 of 4 updated replicas are available...
deployment "web-app-clusterip" successfully rolled out

$ kubectl get rs -l app=web-clusterip
NAME                           DESIRED   CURRENT   READY   AGE
web-app-clusterip-66865d4855   4         4         4       10s

### Scaling the ReplicaSet directly does not stick - the Deployment owns it and puts it back
$ kubectl scale rs web-app-clusterip-66865d4855 --replicas=1

$ kubectl get rs -l app=web-clusterip
NAME                           DESIRED   CURRENT   READY   AGE
web-app-clusterip-66865d4855   4         4         4       13s
